Regional intelligence
PALScout

PAL compatibility and PAL collecting are not the same question.

A French, German or Spanish PAL copy may work perfectly on UK PAL hardware without being a UK-market copy. PALScout keeps those questions separate.

Launch compatibility target: a standard, unmodified UK/European console of the matching platform. Modified consoles, import adapters and bypass methods are outside the beta verdict.
Will it run on my console?

PALScout separates PAL-family evidence from NTSC-U and NTSC-J signals and returns a clear compatible, incompatible or unconfirmed verdict.

Is it actually a UK copy?

Hardware compatibility does not automatically make a game UK-specific. Country and shared-PAL release evidence are reported separately.

Is the packaging/language right for me?

A compatible European game can still have French, German, Spanish or Italian packaging. Language and package evidence remain visible.

What if the listing is unclear?

PALScout returns “needs review” when the evidence is weak or conflicting instead of converting uncertainty into a confident guess.

FAQ

What the beta can — and cannot — tell you.

How do I use RetroNomad today?

Open the listing analyser and paste a listing URL or listing text. You can also upload listing photos. RetroNomad uses the available evidence to identify the game, platform, regional release, edition and completeness.

Does PAL-compatible mean it is a UK copy?

No. A European PAL copy can be compatible with UK hardware while belonging to another country's market or using different packaging/language. PALScout reports compatibility and release identity separately.

Does RetroNomad guarantee compatibility?

No. The result is evidence-dependent. Unknown or conflicting evidence is held for review. The launch verdict assumes standard, unmodified UK/European hardware and does not cover every console revision, modification or import workaround.

Can it tell me whether a listing is a good deal?

Not reliably yet. The exact-release pricing layer is being developed and will only be enabled with a trustworthy, licensed pricing source. RetroNomad will not fabricate a market value when one is unavailable.

Does it automatically search eBay?

Not currently. Users can submit listings manually. Future marketplace discovery will use authorised or otherwise permitted access; the classifier is deliberately marketplace-independent.

Will it support US and Japanese games?

That is the long-term plan. RetroNomad is the global brand; PALScout is the first regional intelligence layer for the UK and European market.
