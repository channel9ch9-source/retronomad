# GrailRaven — AI Differentiation, Messaging and Future B2B Distribution

Recorded: 19 October 2026

Status: **Strategy / commercial hypothesis**, not a product feature launch, confirmed defensible advantage, contracted AI integration or authorisation to sublicense data.

Read alongside `BUSINESS_DIRECTION.md`, `PRODUCT_VISION.md`, `ROADMAP.md`, `PROJECT_STATE.md`, and provider licensing records. The repo is the durable decision source.

## The threat and decision

General AI shopping assistants, marketplace-native search, and price/availability alerts can increasingly perform ordinary title searches and summarise available products. **A retro-game search interface by itself is not a durable moat.** GrailRaven must demonstrate measurably better correct-copy discovery, release evidence and follow-through than a generic AI prompt or saved marketplace search.

The commercial wedge stays **exact-copy acquisition intelligence**, not a general collection manager or a thin AI chat wrapper. `Search → Filter → Compare → Alert` remains the intended customer journey. Preserve `MATCH / REVIEW / FILTERED` uncertainty controls; a seller claim or plausible game title does not establish an exact physical edition.

## Four candidate areas to outperform general-purpose AI

1. **Verified exact physical releases** — structured, evidence-led verification of platform, market/region, original vs reissue/promo, variant, packaging/language, identifiers, completeness and seller photos. Provide clear provenance and uncertainty. “Verified” must be earned per listing or release by sufficient actual evidence, never inferred from catalogue breadth or generated AI text.
2. **Authorised marketplace coverage** — diverse legitimate, fresh inventory sources unified by game/release, conditions, real stock status and relevant shipping/price constraints. Value depends on winning actual permissioned partnerships and proven coverage; general AI services can secure feeds too.
3. **Continuous exact-copy monitoring** — durable Saved Hunts with precise release/edition/completeness/delivered-price requirements, deduplication, safe change detection and valuable, timely alerts, once an authorised live-stock source and working notification delivery are operating. A generic reminder is not a differentiated alert.
4. **Specialist evidence and pricing intelligence** — proprietary/independently validated identifier mappings, release decision rules, confidence and source provenance, plus release-specific reference valuations **only when appropriately licensed**. This means real data quality, not claiming ownership of IGDB records or unrestricted rights to provider pricing/retailer listings.

None of these advantages is automatic or exclusive. Benchmarks must test the collector's actual result: correct matching copies found per search, false MATCH rate, correctly rejected variants, stock freshness, source coverage, delivered-price accuracy, alert precision/time-to-match, time saved, repeat usage and willingness to pay. Compare against manual marketplace search, marketplace-native saved searches and leading AI shopping assistants on the **same test cases and dates**. Avoid hollow “better than AI” marketing without evidence.

## Proposed brand messages

**Core brand phrase / customer-facing short tagline (preferred):**
> Find the right copy. Know why it matches.

This emphasises release evidence without implying every live result is already certified. It complements the existing “Find the right copy at the right price.”

**Short use-case line describing the intended full product (future-facing):**
> GrailRaven helps physical-game collectors discover the exact release they want across authorised marketplaces, check the evidence, compare like-for-like prices and get alerted when matching copies appear.

**Simple social post (accurate to the current partial product):**
> Hunting a specific PS1, PS2 or Dreamcast release? Explore GrailRaven's game catalogue, search available US marketplace candidates and inspect seller listings with our evidence-led checker. The wider exact-copy hunting and alerts experience is in development. grailraven.com

**Partner/investor one-liner — explicitly a company building this capability:**
> GrailRaven is building an exact-copy acquisition-intelligence platform for physical games: release-level verification, authorised multi-marketplace discovery, comparable pricing and persistent hunts — with a potential future B2B verification API for shopping tools and AI agents.

**Longer investor explanation:**
> Collectors care about the physical release, not just a game title; ordinary search and general-purpose AI can confuse territories, editions and completeness. GrailRaven aims to maintain specialist evidence linking catalogue identities and physical identifiers to legally sourced live offers, explain confident matches and uncertainty, then monitor precise requests over time. An initial consumer subscription model may be supplemented, if validated and licensed, by paid retailer integrations and API access for AI-powered commerce systems.

Brand copy rules: do not say all matches are verified, alerts are live, global inventory is comprehensive, or a third-party AI is using GrailRaven until proven. `@grailravengames` Instagram/X are current social handles.

## Optional second distribution and revenue model — “GrailRaven intelligence for AI and merchants”

A useful GrailRaven service could eventually supply structured exact-release *answers* to third-party AI shopping assistants, collector applications, price-checking tools and retro retailers. Example: a permitted API accepts a console, title, intended territory/edition and observed listing identifiers; returns candidate release identities, evidence, `MATCH/REVIEW/FILTERED`, confidence and explanations, plus authorised listing links or licensed price references **only when the relevant contracts permit redistribution and end-user display**.

Potential payment models (hypotheses, no clients or rates agreed):
- **Metered B2B verification API** — charge a client by successful verification/evaluation request or usage band.
- **Monthly B2B SaaS/subscription** — a retailer or software company pays for a quota, reliability and ongoing evidence updates.
- **Merchant/dealer integration/white-label tools** — charge setup and/or subscription for edition-aware listing checks, inventory validation or embedded search features.
- **Permitted partner referral/attribution** — earn commissions on qualifying buyer handoffs to merchants, subject to provider/platform terms and transparent attribution. Do not assume all client traffic sends users back to GrailRaven.
- **Commercial research or licensed derived insights** — only when based on rights-cleared aggregated data, with explicit scope and privacy controls.

Business/site benefits if adopted:
1. New distribution: customers encounter GrailRaven's specialist intelligence inside tools they already use; potentially more qualified traffic when attribution and linkback are permitted.
2. Revenue diversification: sell utility to companies without requiring every collector to visit the GrailRaven site or subscribe personally.
3. Feedback loop: paying integrations create tests of valuable release/edition coverage and reveal missing evidence, helping improve the consumer product if data rights/privacy allow.
4. Potential resilience to general AI shopping: GrailRaven becomes a source some AI systems depend on rather than competing on chat UI alone.

Risks and limits:
- **No automatic sale/integration**: AI products will not discover, call, endorse or pay for the API merely because it exists. Client acquisition, integrations, contracts, billing, support, reliability, uptime and abuse controls are substantial costs.
- **Upstream rights are decisive**: IGDB's provisional go-ahead to use its API/data while awaiting DocuSign is not authority to sublicense/raw-redistribute records. PriceCharting's approved consumer/launch use is not general redistribution/AI resale permission. Each retailer/eBay inventory/photo/feed licence may restrict caching, derivative publication, sharing, public display and commercial API customers. Separate *first-party original release intelligence* from source-licensed fields and examine each agreement with a rights matrix before any B2B service.
- **Privacy/security and competition**: never resell users' private hunts, uploaded listing text/photos or personal data. Rate-limit and authenticate access. B2B margins must exceed inference, provider, operations, sales and customer-support costs. API exposure can cannibalise site usage if pricing/linkback design is weak.
- **Geography and data quality**: PALScout currently has deep evidence for only 100 launch game/platform targets. 8,462 live IGDB catalogue records are not 8,462 verified exact physical releases. US PriceCharting offers are REVIEW only. Do not expose false precision through API.
- Potential API requests to compare/verify do **not** imply real-time source coverage unless inventory/stock freshness are genuinely available.

## Sequencing and concrete future validation gates

**Now — no diversion from core development:**
1. Obtain and benchmark incremental **written-authorised** retailer feeds (Chillout Games, eStarland, RetroFix pending); do not scrape or bypass eBay eligibility.
2. Improve independent per-release evidence and authentic MATCH/REVIEW/FILTERED quality, while keeping 8,462-game published catalogue unchanged and formal IGDB rights review pending.
3. Complete a valuable end-to-end `Search → Filter → Compare → Alert` experience with legal inventory and reliable notification delivery. Measure actual collector utility, return use and willingness to pay.
4. Test against AI and marketplace-native tools on identical realistic target queries, then pursue paid consumer conversion only if the difference matters.

**Later — B2B pilot only after proof:**
5. Identify a *narrow, first-party, rights-clear* service (e.g. physical release evidence matching, with unresolved results as REVIEW), and document field-level provenance and end-user display/sublicensing terms.
6. Interview a handful of potential retailers, commerce-tool developers and AI-tool builders. Seek a real problem and willingness to pay **before** building a general public API.
7. Prototype private authenticated access for 1–2 deliberately approved pilot customers using only rights-cleared fields, with measured accuracy, request costs and support requirements.
8. Charge and evaluate net margins and traffic/referrals; expand only if there is a commercially justified repeatable customer channel. The B2C website must remain useful on its own.

**Standing decision:** B2B/AI distribution is a credible *option*, not an immediate second launch. Prioritise the inventory bottleneck and income-producing collector utility. No new provider spend, marketing launch, API/public integration, dataset promotion, Cloudflare/D1 change, licence interpretation or outreach is authorised by this document.
